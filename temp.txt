  Formula: T(k) = 3k² + 12k + 6

  Two structural phases:

  ---
  Phase 1 — Approach: 3k² − 2k + 1

  Mouse takes k steps from position 2k to center k. Cat starts at 0.

  Step 1 (mouse 2k→2k−1, cat only at 0):
  - 1 initial mouse.turn
  - 1 mouse.move
  - 1 cat.turn
  - 2 cat moves (from 0: stay or go to 1)
  - 2 mouse.turn for new cat positions {0,1}
  = 7 transitions

  Step i (2 ≤ i ≤ k−1, cat can be at positions 0..i−1):
  - i mouse.move (one per cat position)
  - i cat.turn
  - 2 + 3(i−1) = 3i−1 cat moves (pos 0 has 2, rest have 3)
  - (i+1) mouse.turn for new positions
  = 6i transitions

  Step k (final step to center, no cat.turn needed):
  - k mouse.move
  = k transitions

  Total approach = 7 + 6(2+…+(k−1)) + k = 7 + 6·(k(k−1)/2 − 1) + k = 3k² − 2k + 1

  ---
  Phase 2 — Steady state: 14k + 5

  Mouse stays at center forever. Cat reachable at all 2k+1 positions.

  For each cat position c ∈ {0..2k}, one loop:
  - mouse.turn, mouse.move, safe, cat.turn = 4 fixed
  - moves(c) cat moves: 2 for endpoints, 3 for interior

  Total fixed = (2k+1)·4 = 8k+4
  Total cat moves = 2 + 3·(2k−1) + 2 = 6k+1

  Steady = 8k+4 + 6k+1 = 14k + 5

  CM = lambda n, k: S(n, k) + A(n, k)
